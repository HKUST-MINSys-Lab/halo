# Retired dataset converters

The executable authority for active data roles is
`data/scripts/curate/deployment_policy.py`. A converter directory not present in the supervised,
sealed, or prospective rosters is retained only to reproduce historical work. Its presence does
not authorize it for a new run.

Retired payloads remain ignored and may be removed locally once no historical reproduction needs
them. A retired source can return only through a reviewed policy change, a current source/citation
record, converter tests, and a fresh quality audit.
